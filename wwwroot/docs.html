1. Public Landing & Client Showcase

High-conversion luxury salon marketing portal, service catalog, team introduction, verified client reviews, and direct reservation entry point.

Screen 1.1 Hero Section & Primary Booking Call to Action
Route: /
Figure 1.1: Hero banner showcasing luxury brand identity, navigation bar, and primary "Book Reservation" CTA button.
Functionality Description

Serves as the primary digital storefront for SalonSuite. It introduces the salon's luxury aesthetic, brand statement, and provides immediate access to online reservations, service menus, client loyalty lookup, and staff portals via the top navigation bar.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Click "Book an Appointment" or "Reserve Your Ritual" to immediately jump into the 5-step booking engine.
  • Click navigation links (Services, Stylists, Reviews, Contact) for smooth anchor scrolling.
  • Click "Portals / Login" in the top header to access role-based staff or client account dashboards.
Screen 1.2 Featured Treatments & Service Catalog Showcase
Route: /#services
Figure 1.2: Interactive treatment catalog displaying service duration, pricing in Philippine Peso (PHP), and quick book actions.
Functionality Description

Presents the salon's signature service menu categorized into Hair Styling, Balayage & Color, Spa & Scalp Treatments, and Nail Care. Each service card highlights estimated duration (mins), baseline pricing (₱), and brief description.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Browse categorized service offerings with transparent pricing and time estimates.
  • Click the "Book This Service" button on any service card to pre-select that service and open the online booking form.
Screen 1.3 Master Stylists & Creative Directors Showcase
Route: /#stylists
Figure 1.3: Specialist profiles highlighting individual experience, creative specializations, and booking availability.
Functionality Description

Introduces the salon's resident master stylists and specialists, showcasing their profile portraits, artistic titles, years of industry experience, and customer star ratings.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Review each specialist's bio, signature techniques, and guest review ratings.
  • Click "Book with [Stylist Name]" to initiate an appointment pre-assigned to the chosen specialist.
Screen 1.4 Verified Guest Reviews & Testimonials
Route: /#reviews
Figure 1.4: Social proof carousel presenting verified guest feedback, service ratings, and client recommendations.
Functionality Description

Builds customer trust through authenticated feedback from recent salon clients, displaying verified customer names, 5-star quality ratings, and detailed testimonials of their salon experience.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Scroll through reviews to learn about specific treatments and service satisfaction.
Screen 1.5 Salon Operating Hours, Location & Footer Information
Route: /#contact
Figure 1.5: Footer containing business hours, address, direct telephone, email support, and social media links.
Functionality Description

Displays essential branch logistics including studio address, phone numbers, operating schedules (Monday–Sunday 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM), and links to terms, privacy policies, and administrative staff login portals.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Verify studio open hours prior to walk-in visits.
  • Click telephone or email links to directly contact the front desk concierge.

2. Client Online Booking Engine

End-to-end appointment scheduling transaction workflow featuring category browsing, stylist allocation, interactive time slot selection, and automated reservation confirmation.

Screen 2.1 Category Filtering & Service Selection Grid
Route: /book
Figure 2.1: Category filter tabs (Hair, Color, Treatments, Nails) and service selection cards.
Functionality Description

The initial step of the booking transaction. Users filter through treatment categories and select one or more services to add to their booking itinerary.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Click on category tabs (e.g. "Hair Cut & Style", "Coloring", "Spa") to filter available treatments.
  • Click "Select & Book" on the chosen service card to trigger the reservation modal.
Screen 2.2 Interactive Reservation Form & Time Slot Picker
Modal: Reservation Form
Figure 2.2: Reservation modal displaying Specialist selector, date picker, real-time available time slots, and client contact inputs.
Functionality Description

Coordinates specialist allocation, calendar date selection, and appointment time slot validation. Prevents double-booking by only showing currently vacant time slots for the selected stylist.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Select preferred stylist from the dropdown (or select "Any Available Specialist").
  • Choose appointment date using the date picker.
  • Click on an active time slot chip (e.g. "10:00 AM", "02:00 PM").
  • Enter Client Full Name, Mobile Contact Number, Email, and optional Special Requests.
  • Click "Confirm & Complete Reservation".
Screen 2.3 Appointment Summary & Confirmation Success Modal
Modal: Booking Complete
Figure 2.3: Successful booking confirmation banner displaying reference number (APPT-XXXX), selected service, assigned stylist, and time.
Functionality Description

Displays transaction completion state with a unique appointment code, appointment summary breakdown, estimated total cost, and options to print, copy reference, or view in client portal.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Note or copy the generated Appointment Reference ID.
  • Click "View in Client Portal" to check status or "Book Another Service" to return to the catalog.

3. Authentication & Security Gateways

Unified multi-role sign-in system supporting Administrators, Cashiers, Stylists, and Clients with Firebase authentication and quick role-switch test presets.

Screen 3.1 Unified Multi-Role Login Screen
Route: /login
Figure 3.1: Authentication screen featuring Role selector tabs, Email/Password login form, and Quick-login role presets.
Functionality Description

Secure gateway providing access control. Users select their appropriate role (Client, Stylist, Cashier, Admin/Owner) and enter credentials to authenticate and automatically redirect to their dedicated portal.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Select user role tab (Client, Stylist, Cashier, Admin).
  • Enter Email and Password, then click "Sign In to SalonSuite".
  • Alternatively, click any one of the "Quick Demo Login" buttons to test specific role permissions instantly.
Screen 3.2 New Client Account Registration
Route: /login?tab=register
Figure 3.2: Registration form with input validation for Full Name, Email, Mobile Phone, and Password confirmation.
Functionality Description

Enables new salon guests to register an account, automatically enrolling them into the SalonSuite VIP Loyalty Club with a baseline starter balance of 50 welcome points.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Fill in Full Name, Email Address, Contact Number, and Password.
  • Click "Create My Account" to register and enter the Client Portal.

4. Client & Loyalty Rewards Portal

Screen 4.1 Loyalty Member Search & Tier Status Overview
Route: /client-portal
Figure 4.1: Member lookup search bar and VIP tier ranking cards (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond).
Functionality Description

Allows guests or front-desk staff to look up loyalty profiles by Phone Number or Client Name to inspect active rewards points and VIP tier privileges.

How Users Interact With It:

Screen 4.2 Client Account Summary & Points Balance Card

Displays authenticated client metrics, including active VIP tier, lifetime expenditure, total appointments attended, and real-time spendable point balance (1 pt = ₱1 discount).

How Users Interact With It:
  • Review points progress toward next VIP Tier upgrade.
  • Click "Book New Appointment" to schedule a new visit using member credentials.
Screen 4.3 Client Appointment History & Status Ledger
Section: Booking History
Figure 4.3: Filterable table showing past and upcoming appointments, assigned stylist, service type, and live status badge.

Comprehensive ledger of all appointments booked by the client, displaying status badges (Pending, Confirmed, In Service, Completed, Cancelled) and historical receipts.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Filter appointments by status using the dropdown filter.
  • View appointment reference codes, scheduled times, and total prices.
Screen 4.4 Exclusive Loyalty Rewards & Voucher Catalog
Section: Rewards Catalog
Figure 4.4: Points redemption cards offering complimentary scalp massages, discounts, and retail vouchers.
Functionality Description

Presents redeemable reward offers that clients can claim using their accumulated loyalty points during their next salon checkout.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Browse rewards based on required points cost.
  • Click "Redeem Reward" to generate a discount voucher code applied directly to cashier billing.

5. Stylist Terminal & Station Workflow

Dedicated employee workstation for tracking daily appointment queues, executing real-time service state transitions (Seat Client ➔ Service Completed), and reviewing personal commission earnings.

Screen 5.1 Daily Appointment Queue & Station Dashboard
Route: /employee-portal
Figure 5.1: Stylist workstation showing current queue, client notes, scheduled times, and station action triggers.
Functionality Description

Empowers specialists to manage their chair in real time. Stylists select their name to load their personalized daily appointment list, guest preferences, and status action buttons.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Select your Stylist profile from the top selector dropdown.
  • Review assigned clients, scheduled start times, and special styling requests.
Screen 5.2 In-Chair State Transition: "Seat Client" Trigger
Action: Seat Client
Figure 5.2: Appointment card updating to emerald "In Service" status badge with live timer.
Functionality Description

Transaction trigger that transitions the client from "Waiting" to "In Service". This immediately updates the Cashier Floor Queue and Admin live dashboard.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Click the "Seat Client" button when the guest sits at the styling station.
  • Observe status badge change to active "In Service".
Screen 5.3 Service Completion & Hand-Off to Billing
Action: Complete Ritual
Figure 5.3: Card transitioning to "Completed" with automated prompt forwarding client to Cashier POS.
Functionality Description

Signals the conclusion of treatment. Marks service as finished and pushes the completed ticket into the Cashier POS Terminal for final settlement and payment collection.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Click "Complete Ritual" once treatment and styling are finished.
  • Escort client to the front reception desk for checkout.
Screen 5.4 Stylist Commission & Performance Analytics
Tab: Performance & Earnings
Figure 5.4: Performance tab showing Daily Revenue Generated, Commission Rate (%), Total Earnings, and Client Satisfaction Score.
Functionality Description

Provides transparent earnings visibility for stylists, calculating real-time commission earnings based on completed treatments and retail product upsells.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Click the "Performance & Earnings" tab to inspect daily and weekly commissions.
Screen 5.5 Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Ritual Guide
Tab: Ritual Guide (SOP)
Figure 5.5: Comprehensive technical guide outlining step-by-step procedures for balayage, scalp rituals, and sanitized tool prep.
Functionality Description

Standardizes salon service quality by providing in-app technical reference manuals, chemical mixing ratios, exposure times, and hygiene protocols.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Click the "Ritual Guide (SOP)" tab to reference procedural checklists before executing complex chemical or color services.

6. Cashier POS & Floor Operations Terminal

Complete Point of Sale (POS) system for processing client checkouts, retail add-ons, multi-channel payment tenders (Cash, GCash, Maya, Card), change computation, official thermal receipt printing, and floor queue tracking.

Screen 6.1 Active POS Billing & Order Terminal
Route: /cashier
Figure 6.1: Cashier POS terminal displaying quick client selection, service itemization, retail add-ons, and order summary.
Functionality Description

The central billing terminal. Cashiers select completed or walk-in appointments, add retail products (e.g. shampoo, serums) to the cart, apply discounts, and review subtotal, VAT, and grand total.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Select an appointment from the queue to automatically load its service line items.
  • Click "+ Add Retail Product" to attach home-care items sold to the customer.
  • Apply promo vouchers or loyalty points discount if applicable.
Screen 6.2 Multi-Payment Tender Method Selector
Section: Payment Tender
Figure 6.2: Payment method selector supporting Cash, GCash QR, Maya QR, and Credit/Debit Card swipe.
Functionality Description

Supports modern omnichannel payment methods commonly used in Philippine salon operations, including exact cash calculations and digital e-wallet QR verification.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Click on the payment method chip (Cash, GCash, Maya, Card).
  • Enter the Tendered Amount in the payment input field.
Screen 6.3 Automated Change Computation & Payment Finalization
Action: Process Payment
Figure 6.3: Change calculator highlighting exact change due back to client in Philippine Peso.
Functionality Description

Computes exact change due to prevent manual cashier calculation errors, activates the payment confirmation trigger, and generates a formal fiscal invoice record in the database.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Verify the highlighted "Change Due" amount.
  • Click "Finalize & Issue Official Receipt" to complete the transaction.
Screen 6.4 Printable Thermal Official Receipt Modal
Modal: Thermal Receipt
Figure 6.4: High-fidelity 80mm thermal receipt preview with BIR tax breakdown, invoice number, and loyalty points earned.
Functionality Description

Generates an authentic 80mm thermal receipt formatted for POS receipt printers. Features salon TIN, invoice series number, line item breakdown, 12% VAT breakdown, and new loyalty point balance earned from the transaction.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Click "Print Receipt (ESC/POS)" to send ticket to connected receipt printer.
  • Click "New Transaction" to reset the POS terminal for the next guest.
Screen 6.5 Live Salon Floor Queue & Workstation Monitor
Tab: Floor Queue
Figure 6.5: Real-time floor status monitor showing Waiting, In Chair, and Ready for Billing guest distribution.
Functionality Description

Provides front-desk receptionists and cashiers with live situational awareness across all styling chairs, shampoo stations, and waiting lounge areas.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Click the "Floor Queue" tab to view clients currently in treatment.
  • Click "Seat" or "Checkout" directly from the floor queue cards.
Screen 6.6 Specialist Roster & Station Availability
Tab: Stylist Roster
Figure 6.6: Stylist roster cards showing real-time operational status (Available, With Client, On Break).
Functionality Description

Shows live availability of all salon stylists and assistants to assist receptionists in assigning walk-in clients to unencumbered staff.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Inspect staff availability chips (Green = Available, Amber = Busy).
Screen 6.7 Express Walk-in Client Check-In Form
Tab: Walk-in Check-in
Figure 6.7: Rapid intake form for non-booked guests with immediate stylist assignment.
Functionality Description

Streamlined check-in modal designed for quick front-desk intake of walk-in guests without requiring a full pre-booking process.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Input Walk-in Guest Name, Phone Number, desired service, and assign an available stylist.
  • Click "Check-in Walk-in Guest" to immediately insert the ticket into the live queue.
Screen 6.8 Daily Settled Invoices & Shift Audit Register
Tab: Daily Invoices
Figure 6.8: Shift register summarizing total daily cash, digital payments, total sales, and settled invoices.
Functionality Description

Displays all transactions finalized during the cashier's active shift, facilitating end-of-day register reconciliation and cash drawer balancing.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Review shift totals breakdown (Cash Collected, GCash Collected, Card Collections).
  • Click "View Receipt" on any settled transaction row to reprint invoice.

7. Executive Administration & ERP Suite

Enterprise management portal providing real-time KPI metrics, customer CRM, treatment catalog pricing, specialist staff commissions, retail inventory stock control, vendor directories, promotional campaigns, and financial analytics.

Screen 7.1 Executive ERP Dashboard & Real-Time KPI Overview
Route: /portal (Dashboard)
Figure 7.1: Executive dashboard displaying Gross Daily Revenue, Completed Services, Active Specialists, and Low Stock inventory alerts.
Functionality Description

Top-level command center for salon owners and general managers. Highlights revenue performance, real-time appointments volume, labor utilization, and urgent stock notifications.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Inspect KPI cards for today's gross revenue vs. monthly targets.
  • Click any quick-action card to navigate directly into inventory, customers, or reports.
Screen 7.2 Client Relationship Management (CRM) Directory
Route: /portal (Customers)
Figure 7.2: Comprehensive CRM database tracking customer contact details, lifetime spend, visits, and VIP tier.
Functionality Description

Customer intelligence database containing all registered clients. Displays individual customer lifetime value (LTV), visit frequencies, loyalty points, and notes for customized service.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Search customers by name, email, or phone number.
  • Click "+ New Customer Profile" to manually register a VIP guest.
Screen 7.3 Service Menu, Pricing & Category Configuration
Route: /portal (Services)
Figure 7.3: Treatment manager showing service names, categories, durations, baseline pricing, and active status switches.
Functionality Description

Enables salon management to create new services, update prices, adjust service durations (affecting the booking calendar), and toggle availability of seasonal treatments.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Click "+ Add New Treatment" to create a new service offering.
  • Edit existing pricing or duration inline and click "Save Changes".
Screen 7.4 Specialist Staff Directory & Commission Rates
Route: /portal (Stylists)
Figure 7.4: Staff administration cards detailing stylist roles, specializations, commission percentage rates, and active employment status.
Functionality Description

Staff management module for onboarding new stylists, updating individual commission rates (e.g. 15%, 20%), assigning specialties, and managing profile credentials.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Click "+ Add Specialist" to register a new salon employee.
  • Adjust individual commission percentage (%) to update automated earnings calculation.
Screen 7.5 Retail Inventory & SKU Stock Level Tracker
Route: /portal (Inventory)
Figure 7.5: Inventory ledger displaying SKU codes, stock quantity bars, cost price, retail price, and reorder warnings.
Functionality Description

Inventory control system tracking professional back-bar supplies and retail hair-care products. Automatically flags low-stock items falling below reorder thresholds.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Inspect stock level indicators (Red = Low Stock alert).
  • Click "+ Add New SKU" or click "Restock" to increment inventory units.
Screen 7.6 Vendor & Supplier Directory Management
Route: /portal (Suppliers)
Figure 7.6: Supplier cards showing authorized distributors (L'Oréal Professional, Olaplex, Kerastase), lead times, and contact reps.
Functionality Description

Supplier relationship manager for maintaining vendor contacts, delivery terms, payment conditions, and purchase order communications.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Review vendor catalog offerings, representative emails, and contact numbers.
  • Click "+ Add Supplier" to record a new distributor.
Screen 7.7 Promotional Campaigns & Voucher Engine
Route: /portal (Promotions)
Figure 7.7: Promo code engine displaying active discount campaigns (e.g. SUMMERGLOW, LUXURYVIP, FIRSTVISIT) and redemption limits.
Functionality Description

Marketing engine allowing administrators to deploy percentage-based or fixed-value discount codes that integrate directly into online booking and cashier POS billing.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Click "+ Create Campaign Code" to deploy a new promotion with start/end expiry dates.
Screen 7.8 Loyalty Points Rules & VIP Tier Rewards Config
Route: /portal (Loyalty)
Figure 7.8: Loyalty point system config showing earn multipliers, points-to-cash conversion rates, and tier thresholds.
Functionality Description

Configures the business logic governing customer loyalty points, defining how many points are earned per ₱100 spent and establishing minimum spend requirements for Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Diamond VIP tiers.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Adjust reward redemption costs and tier multipliers.
  • Click "Update Loyalty Parameters" to commit changes.
Screen 7.9 Financial Audit Ledger & Invoices Repository
Route: /portal (Invoices)
Figure 7.9: Searchable sales invoice ledger displaying invoice serials, dates, customers, payment methods, and net revenues.
Functionality Description

Centralized financial audit log capturing every monetary transaction finalized in the salon for accounting, tax compliance, and revenue reporting.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Filter records by date range, payment method (Cash/GCash/Card), or client name.
  • Click on an invoice row to inspect complete receipt breakdown.
Screen 7.10 Financial Performance & Revenue Trends Analytics
Route: /portal (Analytics Top)
Figure 7.10: Interactive analytics graphs showing weekly revenue trends, category sales distributions, and revenue growth.
Functionality Description

Visual business intelligence module displaying interactive chart data on daily revenue surges, service vs. retail sales ratios, and overall monthly financial health.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Hover over trend lines to view exact daily gross sales.
  • Select date filter presets (Last 7 Days, This Month, Year to Date).
Screen 7.11 Top Stylists & Payment Method Breakdown Charts
Route: /portal (Analytics Bottom)
Figure 7.11: Specialist revenue ranking leaderboard and doughnut chart of payment channel distributions.
Functionality Description

Reveals staff productivity rankings (top revenue earners) and transaction share across payment channels (Cash vs. GCash vs. Maya vs. Cards) to assist in financial planning.

How Users Interact With It:
  • Review top stylists leaderboard to calculate bonuses and incentives.
  • Analyze payment method shares for cashless transaction optimization.
